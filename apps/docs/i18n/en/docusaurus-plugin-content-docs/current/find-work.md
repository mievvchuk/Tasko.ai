## Marketplace

Open **Find work** (the [marketplace](https://taskoai.pp.ua/marketplace)). It lists only projects that are looking for freelancers right now: new ones or those where the client is still building a team (marked "Building a team").

Filters at the top:

- **Search** by title and description;
- **Category** and **Skill**;
- **Budget from / to**, UAH;
- **Favorites only** — projects you starred.

Filters are kept in the page address, so you can share a selection as a link.

## Applying

1. Open a project card to read the description, budget, deadline and skills.
2. Click **Apply**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Cover message** (10 to 2000 characters): your experience and how exactly you will do the work;
   - **Budget**, UAH;
   - **Timeline** in days (1 to 365).
4. Click **Send application**.

Each application gets one answer from the client: **Accepted** or **Rejected**. The status is shown on the project card and in [notifications](./notifications-workload.md).

## Messaging the client

While the application awaits a decision, the project card has a **Message the client** link: you can clarify details before being hired. Messages arrive instantly.

## After being hired

The project appears in **Work projects**. The project page has a **Task queue** block: click **Take the next task**, and Tasko.ai gives you a free task with the nearest deadline and higher priority. You can take a new one once the previous one is sent for review.

:::tip
Clients more often choose freelancers with a complete [profile](./profile-security.md), a portfolio and honest availability.
:::
