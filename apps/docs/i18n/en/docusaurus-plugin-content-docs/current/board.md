## Columns

The board shows project tasks by status:

**To do → In progress → In review → Done**

- Cards can be dragged manually between **To do** and **In progress**.
- A task moves to **In review** when the freelancer [submits work](./submit-work.md).
- **Done** is set by the client when they accept the result.

Inside the "To do" and "In progress" stages you can add **custom columns**, e.g. "Design" or "Layout", and drag them to change the order.

## New task

Click **Create task** on the project page and fill in:

- **Title** and **description** — what exactly to do and how to check the result;
- **Assignee** from the team members;
- **Priority**: low, medium or high;
- **Deadline**;
- the **milestone** the task belongs to and a **rating** from 1 to 5, if needed.

## Risk marks

Cards highlight risks:

- **Overdue** — the deadline has passed and the task isn't done;
- **Deadline soon** — only a few days left;
- **High priority**.

## The Tasks page

[Tasks](https://taskoai.pp.ua/tasks) collects your tasks from all projects: search, filters by project, status, priority and assignee. For a freelancer it is the main to-do list for the day.
