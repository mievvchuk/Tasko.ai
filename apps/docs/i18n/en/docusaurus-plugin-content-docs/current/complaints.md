A complaint lets you tell an administrator that someone breaks the [platform rules](./kb-rules.md) or a project looks fraudulent.

## Filing

1. Open [Complaints](https://taskoai.pp.ua/complaints).
2. Choose the target: a **person** or a **project**.
3. Describe the problem with facts: what happened, when and where it is visible (chat, task, file).
4. Send it.

:::tip
A complaint with specifics is reviewed faster. Instead of "he is a bad freelancer" write: "the 'Cart' task is 10 days overdue, no reply to messages since 3 November".
:::

## What's next

- The complaint stays **Awaiting review** until an administrator handles it.
- After the review it becomes **Resolved** (the violation is confirmed and action taken) or **Rejected** (no violation found).

All your complaints and their current statuses are on the same page.
