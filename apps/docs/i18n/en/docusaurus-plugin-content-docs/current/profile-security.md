## Profile

In your [profile](https://taskoai.pp.ua/profile) you can change your name and photo, see your email and sign-up date, and switch your [role](./kb-roles.md).

A **freelancer** also fills in a professional profile — this is what clients see when choosing a freelancer:

- **Skills** — clients and the marketplace use them to match projects.
- **Rate** and **availability**: available, busy or unavailable.
- **Portfolio** — links to previous work.

The **How clients see my profile** button opens the public profile page.

:::tip
A complete profile with a portfolio noticeably raises the chance that your application is accepted.
:::

## Theme and language

The theme (light, dark or system) and language (Ukrainian or English) switches are at the top of the side panel. Your choice is saved in the browser.

## Sign-in and "Remember me"

- Without **Remember me** the session lasts 1 hour and ends when you close the tab.
- With it, you stay signed in on this device for **30 days**. Don't use it on someone else's computer.
- After 10 failed sign-in attempts in a row, signing in is blocked for 15 minutes.

## Sign in with Google

The sign-in and sign-up pages have a **Sign in with Google** (**Sign up with Google**) button.

- If there is no account with that email yet, Tasko.ai creates one with the name and email from Google. You pick the role on the welcome page, as after a normal sign-up.
- If an account with that email already exists, Google is linked to it: from then on you can sign in with the password or with Google.
- An account created with Google has no password. You can **set** one in the security block of your profile to sign in with your email as well.
- If two-factor sign-in is on, the app code is still requested after Google.
- **Remember me** works for Google sign-in too.

## Two-factor sign-in (2FA)

Two-factor sign-in protects the account even if someone learns your password.

1. In the security block of your profile, click **Enable 2FA**.
2. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app: Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password, etc.
3. Enter the six-digit code from the app and click **Confirm 2FA**.

From then on, signing in needs a code from the app in addition to the password. You can turn it off with **Disable 2FA** in the same place.

## Password and sessions

- To change the password, enter the current one and a new one. The new password must differ from the current one. After the change all other active sessions end.
- The **Active sessions** list shows which browsers and devices are signed in. End an unfamiliar session with **End**.
