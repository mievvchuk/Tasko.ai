## Freelancer: submitting

1. Open the task.
2. In the submission block describe what was done and add a link to the result or a file.
3. Click **Send**. The task moves to **In review**, and the client is notified.

:::tip
Explain how to check the result: where to look, what to click, which data to use. The client will accept the work faster.
:::

## Client: reviewing

![A task page with a submission awaiting review and the client's decision form](/img/help/submit-work.en.webp)

1. Open the task in review (it is also in your [notifications](./notifications-workload.md)).
2. Check the result and choose:
   - **Accept** — the task is completed;
   - **Return for revision** — with a comment on what exactly to fix.
3. After the revision the freelancer submits the task again. The resubmission shows why it was returned last time.

## Submission statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Awaiting review** | The freelancer sent the result, the client hasn't answered yet |
| **Accepted** | The client accepted the work |
| **Returned for revision** | Needs fixing and resubmitting |

## Review

When the project is completed, the client can leave a review for each member: a rating from 1 to 5 and a comment. Reviews are shown in the freelancer's profile and affect their rating.
