Tasko.ai is a freelance marketplace and a workspace in one place. A client publishes a project, freelancers apply, and once the team is hired all the work happens right here: tasks on a Kanban board, milestones, submitting and reviewing results, chat, files and the AI assistant **Ada**.

## Sign up

1. Open the [sign-up page](https://taskoai.pp.ua/register).
2. Enter your name, email and a password (8 to 128 characters). The meter under the field shows how strong the password is.
3. After signing up you are logged in right away and see the welcome page.

Instead of an email and password you can click **Sign up with Google** — Tasko.ai takes your name and email from your Google account. See [Profile and security](./profile-security.md#sign-in-with-google).

## Pick a role

On the welcome page Tasko.ai asks what you plan to do:

- **Looking for a freelancer** — you are a **client**: you publish projects and hire freelancers.
- **Looking for work** — you are a **freelancer**: you find projects on the marketplace and complete tasks.

You can change the role later in your [profile](https://taskoai.pp.ua/profile) while you have no projects or applications. More in [Roles on the platform](./kb-roles.md).

## First steps for a client

1. [Create a project](./create-project.md): title, description, budget, deadline and required skills.
2. Wait for an administrator to approve it — then it appears on the marketplace.
3. Review applications, message a candidate if needed and [choose freelancers](./choose-freelancer.md).
4. Plan work on the [Kanban board](./board.md) and [accept results](./submit-work.md).

## First steps for a freelancer

1. [Fill in your profile](./profile-security.md): skills, rate, availability and portfolio. This is what clients see.
2. [Find a project on the marketplace](./find-work.md) and apply with a budget and a timeline.
3. Once hired, take tasks from the queue, complete them and [submit them for review](./submit-work.md).

## Where to find tips

- In the app there is a **?** button in the bottom-right corner — it shows articles for the current screen.
- The **Ada** button next to it opens the [AI assistant](./ai.md), who answers questions about your projects.
- All statuses are explained in [Statuses](./kb-statuses.md), and common questions are answered in the [FAQ](./kb-faq.md).
