## Notifications

[Notifications](https://taskoai.pp.ua/notifications) tell you about important events:

- new applications to your project, your application being accepted or rejected;
- new messages in the project chat and in application messages;
- a newly assigned task, submitted work and the review result;
- new and updated milestones, new members and project changes;
- a project rejected by the moderator and an AI plan being added;
- for administrators — new complaints.

The number next to the bell in the menu is the unread count. Click a notification to jump straight to the right place.

## Workload

[Workload](https://taskoai.pp.ua/workload) shows how many tasks are in progress and which deadlines are coming.

- For a **freelancer** — whether to take new work or first close the current tasks.
- For a **client** — who in the team is overloaded and who should get the next task.

:::tip
If deadlines clash, ask [Ada](./ai.md): "What should I do first this week?"
:::
