Tasko.ai has three roles. The role decides which sections you see in the menu and what you can do.

| Role | What they do | Main sections |
|---|---|---|
| **Client** | Publishes projects, chooses freelancers, sets tasks, accepts work and leaves reviews | My projects, Applications, Board, Milestones |
| **Freelancer** | Finds projects on the marketplace, applies, completes tasks and submits results | Find work, Work projects, Tasks, Workload |
| **Administrator** | Moderates projects, manages users, reviews complaints, sees platform analytics | Admin panel |

## How to pick or change the role

Sign-up does not ask for a role: every new account starts as a client. Right after logging in, the welcome page lets you pick **Looking for a freelancer** (client) or **Looking for work** (freelancer).

Later you can change the role in your [profile](https://taskoai.pp.ua/profile) with the same switch.

:::info Limitation
You can change the role only while you have **no projects, applications or team memberships**. After that the role is fixed so project history stays consistent. Create a separate account for another role.
:::

## Administrator

The administrator role can't be picked by yourself: another administrator grants it in the **Users** section of the admin panel. See [Admin panel](./admin.md).
