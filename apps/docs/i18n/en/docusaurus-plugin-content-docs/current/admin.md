Administrators work in the [admin panel](https://taskoai.pp.ua/admin). It opens right after signing in.

## Overview

The platform's analytics report: the number of users (and blocked ones), projects — active and completed, tasks — by status and overdue, plus the trend of sign-ups, new projects and complaints by day and month.

## Users

- Search by name or email, filters by role and status, sorting by columns.
- **Change role** of a user.
- **Block** or **unblock**: a blocked user can't sign in, their sessions end, the data is kept.
- **Delete**: personal data is anonymized while project history stays intact.
- **New administrator** — create another administrator account.

## Moderation

New and changed projects awaiting a decision.

- **Approve and publish** — the project becomes "Looking for a freelancer" and appears on the marketplace.
- **Reject project** — with a reason the client will see. After fixing it, the project returns to moderation.

## Projects

All projects on the platform with search and a status filter. Each can be opened and viewed but not changed: only the owner and the team edit a project, and messages and files stay private.

## Complaints

User complaints about people and projects. The administrator reviews the details and marks a complaint **Resolved** or **Rejected**; the author sees the new status on the Complaints page. Administrators are notified about new complaints.
