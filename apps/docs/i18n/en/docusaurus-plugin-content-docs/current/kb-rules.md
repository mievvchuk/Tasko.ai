## Communication

- Agree on terms in the project chat or in application messages — the history stays on the platform and helps if a dispute arises.
- Be polite. Insults, threats and spam are grounds for a [complaint](./complaints.md).

## Projects and applications

- Describe real work with a real budget. The moderator rejects fake and fraudulent projects.
- Don't post contacts or personal data in project descriptions and applications.
- Apply only to projects you are ready to complete within the proposed timeline.

## Files

- Work materials only: PDF, PNG, JPG, DOCX, ZIP or TXT, up to 10 MB.
- Don't upload other people's materials without the right to do so.

## Violations

For violations an administrator can reject a project, resolve a complaint against a member or **block the account**. A blocked user can't sign in, and their active sessions end.
