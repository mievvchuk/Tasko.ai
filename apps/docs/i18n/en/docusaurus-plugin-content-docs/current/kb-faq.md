## Account

### I forgot my password — what now?

There is no self-service password reset yet. Contact the platform administrator: they will help you get access back.

### Why do I get signed out?

Without **Remember me** the session lasts 1 hour and ends when the tab is closed. Tick it when signing in to stay signed in for 30 days. See [Profile and security](./profile-security.md).

### Sign-in says "too many attempts".

After 10 failed attempts signing in is blocked for 15 minutes. Wait, then check the email, the password and the keyboard layout.

### Can I change my role?

Yes, in your profile — while you have no projects, applications or team memberships. See [Roles on the platform](./kb-roles.md).

## Projects and the marketplace

### Why isn't my project on the marketplace?

- It is still **in moderation** or was **rejected** — check the status on the project page.
- You have **closed hiring** — then the project leaves the marketplace.
- The project is **paused**, **completed** or **cancelled**.

### Can I hire several freelancers?

Yes. Accept applications one by one: the project stays on the marketplace until you click **Close hiring**. See [How to choose a freelancer](./choose-freelancer.md).

### How do I ask a freelancer something before hiring?

On the applications page click **Message the freelancer** — a conversation about that application opens.

## Work and payment

### Does Tasko.ai process payments?

No. The platform helps agree on and track payment by [milestones](./milestones.md), but the money is transferred outside it.

### Why can't I drag a card to "In review" or "Done"?

These statuses aren't set by dragging: "In review" is set when the freelancer submits work, "Done" when the client accepts it. See [Kanban board](./board.md).

## Ada

### Ada gave a wrong answer.

AI can make mistakes. Rephrase the question or check the data on the project page.

### Where do I find an answer that isn't here?

Ask [Ada](https://taskoai.pp.ua/ai) or message a project member in [messages](https://taskoai.pp.ua/messages).
